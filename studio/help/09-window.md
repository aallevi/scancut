# The window

## Tabs

Tabs sit in the title bar, one source per tab. A click opens in this tab; ⌘-click, middle-click or
right-click (⌃-click) › Open in new tab opens a new one behind, as in a browser: you stay where you are and
the new tab lights up for a moment (with ⇧ you go there at once). ⌘T and + open an empty tab in front. ⌘T new tab, ⌘W close, ⌃Tab next. Each tab keeps its
place, zoom, selection and undo; work keeps running when you switch. Back in a tab opened from
another goes back to it and closes the tab.

## The bar at the bottom

Left to right: the status line, previous and next (← and →: in a gallery and in the library's Sources
and Cuts they move the selection; in the editor they open the scan before or after in its folder, from wherever it was opened; ⌥ jumps to the first
or the last), shuffle (the arrows go to a random one instead, like a player), the round button (in the
editor: scissors, Cut; elsewhere: a scanner, scan the next page), then undo and redo in the editor, or
Back and Forward (⌘[ and ⌘]) elsewhere. A button that can do nothing right now is dimmed.

## Thumbnail size

The two lenses at the top of a gallery and of the library (or ⌘+ and ⌘−) make the thumbnails bigger or
smaller, in steps. The size is remembered.

## Opening a scan

Parts, texts, the side-by-side view and the library details appear at once; the image comes after.
Until the full preview arrives the gallery's thumbnail stands in for it.

## Panes

Click a section's title to fold it (in the sidebar and in the right pane). To give the image all the
room, fold a whole pane away: the small tab on its edge, View › Sidebar / Right pane, ⌥⌘1 and ⌥⌘2.

## Looking closely

- **Image only** (Y, View menu, or the checkbox under the tools): just the image, without parts,
  shading or text boxes. Y again shows them.
- **Loupe** (G): 4, 8 or 16:1, real pixels.
- **Densitometer**: RGB, L\*a\*b\*, distance from the background colour (and whether the pixel counts as
  background or foreground); single pixel, 3×3 or 5×5.
- **Histogram** of the scan or the selected part.
- **Backgrounds** (View): light blue, white, grey, dark, checkerboard.

## Units

Every number shows its unit in grey: tolerance with the real colour distance under it, wand
tolerance (of 255), brush in mm and px, scores from 0 to 1, sizes in mm.

## Search

The search box (top right) finds sources and cuts by name, parts by label and by their text (the
text of the whole part and of every text area read inside it), and help topics. A text found opens
the cut that shows it, named in the result, with the Text pane open: the block with that text is
framed and the words are selected in it, and marked in the side-by-side view. ⌥-click opens the
source with that part selected, as does a part not cut yet. Enter opens the first result; ⌘-click
in a new tab.
