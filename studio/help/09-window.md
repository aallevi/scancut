# The window

## Tabs

Tabs sit in the title bar, one source per tab. A click opens in this tab; ⌘-click, middle-click or
right-click › Open in new tab opens a new one. ⌘T new tab, ⌘W close, ⌃Tab next. Each tab keeps its
place, zoom, selection and undo; work keeps running when you switch. Back in a tab opened from
another goes back to it and closes the tab.

## Panes

Click a section's title to fold it (in the sidebar and in the right pane). To give the image all the
room, fold a whole pane away: the small tab on its edge, View › Sidebar / Right pane, ⌥⌘1 and ⌥⌘2.

## Looking closely

- **Image only** (Y, View menu, or the checkbox under the tools): just the image, without parts,
  shading or text boxes. Y again shows them.
- **Loupe** (G): 4, 8 or 16:1, real pixels.
- **Densitometer**: RGB, L\*a\*b\*, distance from the background colour (and whether the pixel counts as
  background or foreground); single pixel, 3×3 or 5×5.
- **Histogram** of the scan or the selected part.
- **Backgrounds** (View): light blue, white, grey, dark, checkerboard.

## Units

Every number shows its unit in grey: tolerance with the real colour distance under it, wand
tolerance (of 255), brush in mm and px, scores from 0 to 1, sizes in mm.

## Search

The search box (top right) finds sources and cuts by name, parts by label and by their text, and
help topics. Enter opens the first result; ⌘-click in a new tab.
