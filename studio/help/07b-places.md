# Where things are kept

The library knows where every sheet of paper is kept, the same way as the archive site: a **place**
keeps things and other places (a chest of drawers, a drawer, a box, a folder, or a work that keeps
things, like a book). Every sheet is in **one place only**, with a **number** there. Its **address** is
the numbers from the outside in: `1 · 24 · 5 · 3`.

## Rules

The library keeps these by itself, so nothing needs checking by hand:

- Numbers belong to the place: they run through the whole place, places and things together. Two
  things in one place never share a number.
- A place cannot be inside itself, and a place that still keeps things cannot be deleted.
- A number can be left empty: the archive gives the next free one.
- Every move is written in the history.

## Placing

Select a source in the library: **Kept in** sets the place and category for the whole source, and
each part can have its own place, number and category (otherwise it is "same as the source"). You can
also type an address: `24-05` (box 24, folder 5) or `1 · 24 · 5`. The pieces of a cut cut again belong
to their own item and to the source they came from, and are kept where their part is.

**Places** in the sidebar lists every place with how many things it keeps; click one to see only
those sources and cuts. **+ New place…** makes one.

## Categories

The archive's categories (Drawing, Document, Digital photograph, Book…), for a whole source or one part.
