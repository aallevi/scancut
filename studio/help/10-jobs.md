# Jobs and batch

Everything that takes time is a job: scans, detect, cut, read, re-cut, import, downloads. **Jobs** (left)
lists them with real progress; the number beside it counts the running ones. Click a job to go to
what it is working on.

**Batch**: select several sources in a gallery or in Library › Sources; the Batch menu and the pane offer the same actions: detect, detect and cut, cut the parts already marked, pass whole, read the cuts or the whole documents, join as one object, a multipage file, the Trash. A scan a job is working on shows a turning ring on its thumbnail, and the view refreshes when the job ends.
